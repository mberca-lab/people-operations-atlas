Demonstrated documentation · proposed operational design
Operational controls
and resolution workflow.
Document reporting rules, investigate recurring exceptions and define source-level corrections, approval requirements and follow-up controls.

  

    
01 / CAPTURE
Preserve the source
Versioned extracts, snapshot hash, source row IDs, field types and effective dates. Minimise restricted employee data.

    
02 / CONTROL
Test the contract
Unique keys, valid critical fields, one-to-one joins, approval authority, lifecycle dates and completeness gates.

    
03 / INVESTIGATE
Trace and assign
Exception → source → root-cause hypothesis → accountable owner. Separate observed evidence from unverified explanations.

    
04 / CLOSE
Verify the correction
Source owner approves correction, independent refresh re-checks the control, reviewer signs off and evidence is retained.

  

  

  

    SOP / EXCEPTION INVESTIGATION & SIGN-OFF
A practical review protocol

    
    
      
  1. Log the observed flag. Record the exception ID, system/row references, reporting date and field-level issue. Keep sensitive case narratives in the restricted case system.
    2. 
      
  2. Validate scope and authority. Confirm identity, employment dates, approved effective-dated records and reporting denominator. Establish the expected payment from an independent approval trail.
    3. 
      
  3. Assign and escalate. People Systems owns identity/master fixes; Payroll owns pay reconciliation; People Operations owns absence/time follow-up; authorised HR/provider specialists own restricted cases.
    4. 
      
  4. Document the explanation. Record observed facts, hypotheses, checks and approved actions separately. Keep the exception open until the supporting source and evidence are verified.
    5. 
      
  5. Correct at source, then refresh. Obtain correction approval and audit evidence, rerun control checks and reconcile totals before recording closure.
    6. 
      
  6. Approve and prevent recurrence. Independent reviewer signs off, retains versioned evidence under the applicable policy and adds a capture/preflight rule. Measure recurrence only after real implementation.

Proposed BambooHR mapping and Power BI model
BambooHR export specification
Employee ID → exact employee key; department → controlled taxonomy; FTE → numeric 0–1; hire/end dates → lifecycle inclusion; full-time annual salary → pay expectation; compensation effective date → approved history. Implementation would require validation with the platform owner.
Proposed Power BI model
Use employee/date/department dimensions and separate payroll, absence, time and case facts at their correct grain. Define approved comparable gross, signed/absolute variance and FTE-weighted absence as measures. Apply role-based access and restricted-case separation. This specification is provided for future implementation; a PBIX file is outside the current deliverables.

Data scope and limitations
Seed 731 · 360 fictional employee keys · seven HR/payroll/approval/absence/time/visa/case feeds · as-of 2026-10-31. All operational records are synthetic. Employee, customer and sensitive case data are excluded.
  • Payroll covers full-month gross; tax, pensions and proration are outside scope.
  • Absence uses weekday/FTE exposure; holiday and custom-roster adjustments require further development.
  • Working-time outputs are operational review indicators. Statutory compliance requires qualified assessment.
  • Visa and ER examples cover case metadata and proposed escalation procedures.
  • Production use requires data-owner authorisation, access controls and HR/payroll/legal approval.
